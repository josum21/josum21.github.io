About

Experimental particle physicist working on the ATLAS experiment at CERN. Currently a postdoctoral researcher (CDD Chercheur) at IJCLab, Université Paris-Saclay, I focus on electroweak physics — vector-boson scattering, differential cross-section measurements and effective-field-theory (EFT) interpretations — alongside detector R&D for future colliders (ALLEGRO / FCC-ee).

I previously completed a PhD in particle physics at the University of Manchester and an undergraduate degree at Nankai University, with analysis experience across ATLAS and BESIII.
